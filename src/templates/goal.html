How it works
Step 1 of 3
Speak your goal

Tell CogAlign what you're working on, out loud or typed. It'll hold onto it for the whole session.

Step 2 of 3
It quietly keeps watch

While you work, CogAlign keeps half an eye on your screen in the background, no popups, no interruptions.

Step 3 of 3
If you drift, CogAlign steps in

The longer you're off track, the more it speaks up, always a message, never a block.

Three ways CogAlign nudges you back on track

First, after 5s
Empathetic Coach

Warm and supportive, speaks to your goal directly.

Then, after 10s
Sarcastic Friend

Witty and direct, calls out the exact distraction.

Finally, after 15s
Deadline Supervisor

Formal and urgent, one direct order to return.
